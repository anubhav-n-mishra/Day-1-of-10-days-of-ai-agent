## 🎯 Introducing: Teach-the-Tutor

An AI-powered active recall learning platform that brings the Feynman Technique to life through voice. Built with LiveKit Agents and powered by Murf's Falcon TTS voices.

### 🌟 What Makes It Special?

**Three Distinct Learning Modes:**

1️⃣ **Learn Mode (Matthew)** 
- Patient, knowledgeable instructor voice
- Explains programming concepts in detail using Google Gemini
- Breaks down complex topics with real-world analogies
- Generates explanations dynamically - no hardcoded content!

2️⃣ **Quiz Mode (Alicia)** 
- Energetic, encouraging quiz instructor
- Creates custom questions on the fly
- Gives specific, constructive feedback
- Makes wrong answers into learning moments

3️⃣ **Teach Back Mode (Ken)** 
- Thoughtful mentor who listens actively
- YOU explain concepts while he evaluates
- Provides detailed feedback on what you got right
- Helps you identify gaps in understanding

**The Secret Sauce:** Each mode uses a different Murf Falcon voice personality, making it feel like talking to three different instructors! Voice switching happens seamlessly during mode transitions.

### 🔧 Technical Stack:
- **Backend:** LiveKit Agents 1.3.2 with multi-agent handoffs
- **STT:** Deepgram Nova-3 (ultra-accurate transcription)
- **LLM:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (dynamic content generation)
- **TTS:** Murf Falcon with 3 distinct voices (Matthew, Alicia, Ken)
- **Turn Detection:** MultilingualModel for natural conversations
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 with Coursera-inspired UI (that iconic #0056D2 blue!)

### 💡 Key Innovations:

✅ **Zero Hardcoded Content** - All explanations, questions, and feedback generated by Gemini in real-time

✅ **Smart Voice Switching** - Manual TTS reconfiguration during agent transfers ensures each mode has its unique voice

✅ **No Duplicate Greetings** - Clean agent transitions without repeated messages

✅ **Automatic Engagement** - Agents greet you immediately when modes start

✅ **Active Recall Built-In** - Proven learning technique powered by voice AI

### 🎨 The Experience:

Imagine opening the app, hearing Matthew say:
> "Welcome to Coursera's Teach-the-Tutor! I'm Matthew, and I'll help you master programming through active recall..."

Then seamlessly switching to quiz mode:
> "Hey there! I'm Alicia, your Quiz Mode instructor! Let's test what you've learned..."

And finally practicing the Feynman Technique:
> "Hello! I'm Ken. Teaching others is the best way to truly master a subject. I'm all ears!"

It's like having three personal tutors available 24/7, accessible through your browser. 🌐

### 🚀 What I Learned:
